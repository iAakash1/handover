# 00 · TIH foundation weeks: image processing and preprocessing notebooks (December 2025)

The day-wise lab notebooks of TIH Week 1 (2–5 Dec) and Week 2 (8–12 Dec), as they were on the Mac
(`~/Desktop/IITB`). The Week-2 functions became the `preprocessing/` package of project 01 and the quality gate
used by every later pipeline.

**Environment:** any Python 3.10+ with `opencv-python numpy matplotlib pandas pillow` (Week 2 Day 4 also needs
`tensorflow`). Open the notebooks with Jupyter from the day's own folder, because they use relative paths.

## Week 1 — image operations, OpenCV vs NumPy from scratch

| Folder | What it does | Outputs kept |
|---|---|---|
| `Week 1/Day 3/day3.ipynb` | 2-D convolution written from scratch (naive and vectorised with `as_strided`), Sobel and Canny edges, sharpening; timed against OpenCV | `before_gray.png`, `after_sobel_cv_3x3.png`, `after_canny.png`, `after_sharpen_opencv.png` |
| `Week 1/Day 4/day4.ipynb` | Batch run over a folder of photos: resize, box blur, sharpen, Sobel edges, emboss, BGR→gray/RGB/HSV, each as an OpenCV call and as NumPy scratch code, with timings | `outputs/day4/package/`, `outputs/day4/scratch/`, `day4_time_details.csv`, `day4_time_summary.csv` |
| `Week 1/Day 4/report.md` | Week 1 lab report (objectives, day-wise summary, findings) | – |

Week 1 Day 1 and Day 2 (read/save, metadata, resize/crop/rotate/flip, interpolation, blurs, colour spaces) have no
folders of their own on the Mac; `report.md` covers all four days.

## Week 2 — training preprocessing, quality checks, augmentation, inference guardrails

| Folder | What it does | Outputs kept |
|---|---|---|
| `Week 2/Day 1/` | `preprocess_for_training()`: optional centre crop, resize to 224×224, BGR→RGB/gray, normalise to 0–1 (`preprocess_for_training.py` is saved as notebook JSON; open it with Jupyter) | `data/` (5 input leaves), `processed_images/` |
| `Week 2/Day 2/` | Quality checks on every training / validation / test image (`quality_checks.py`: blur = variance of Laplacian, brightness, contrast, noise) and offline augmentation (`augmentation.py`) | `quality_report.csv` (all images), `quality_report_low_quality_only.csv`, `data_low_quality/` (the 155 images that failed) |
| `Week 2/Day 3/` | Training-time vs inference-time preprocessing (`preprocessing_functions.py`) | `week 2 day3 Notes.pdf` |
| `Week 2/Day 4/` | `preprocess_for_inference()` with all guardrails (resolution, blur, brightness, contrast, noise, blank image) in front of a MobileNetV2 | `data/` (20 everyday photos used to test the guardrails), `prediction_results.csv` (accepted / rejected per check) |

Week 2 Day 5 (one integrated module) is `projects/01_cnn_keras_tomato/preprocessing/`.
