GENERATION LOG
==============
Run date: 2026-06-23 (round 1, initial captioning)
Input: /mnt/user-data/uploads/caption.zip

STEP 1 — Extraction
  Unzipped caption.zip. Archive contained 45 entries total; after
  excluding __MACOSX/ metadata and .DS_Store files, 16 genuine images
  remained across 4 crop folders:
    cotton/                 5 images
    potato/lateblight/      3 images
    tomato/                 4 images
    bellpepper/bacterial spot/  4 images

STEP 2 — Direct visual inspection
  Each of the 16 images was opened and examined individually before any
  caption text was written. Notes on color, shape/texture, distribution,
  and background context were recorded per image. Filename/folder labels
  were treated as secondary hints only, per the task brief.

STEP 3 — Caption authoring
  5 captions were written per image (80 total), varying length, focus
  (color / shape / distribution / overall impression / comparison-
  context), and sentence style, grounded in the Step 2 notes.

STEP 4 — Structured output
  captions.json and captions.csv generated from the authored records.

STEP 5 — Dataset-level validation (round 1)
  Pairwise similarity + opening-word frequency + manual label-vs-visual
  cross-check. See validation_report.txt.

==============================================================
Run date: 2026-06-23 (round 2, re-audit + technical QC + rewrite)
Input: same caption.zip, re-uploaded with an expanded brief

STEP 6 — Phrase audit against the expanded brief
  Scanned all 80 round-1 captions against the brief's banned-phrase and
  banned-verb lists, plus a new "avoid excessive use of" watch list
  (while, against the, at a glance, most of, could pass for, etc.).
  Found 20 hits across 14 captions (all carried over from round 1,
  before this stricter check existed). Rewrote all 14 — full before/
  after text in rewrite_log.txt. Re-scanned: 0 hits remaining.

STEP 7 — Real technical image QC (new in round 2)
  - Blur/sharpness: variance-of-Laplacian per image -> low_quality_images.txt
  - Near-duplicate detection: 64-bit perceptual hash, Hamming distance
    -> duplicate_images.txt
  - Per-image metadata (dimensions, format, size, hashes) -> metadata.csv
  - Dataset-wide summary (crop/disease counts, augmented-file detection,
    class balance note) -> dataset_analysis.txt
  Method and results documented in full in quality_control_log.txt.

STEP 8 — Internet image expansion: NOT attempted
  The round-2 brief asked for additional images to be sourced from
  PlantVillage/APS/ICAR/FAO/Wikimedia Commons/government portals, with
  a download_sources.csv tracking real URLs. This sandbox has no access
  to those domains and no web-search tool was available in this
  conversation. Rather than fabricate source URLs or invent images,
  this step was skipped entirely — external_images/ and
  download_sources.csv are not part of this delivery. Flagged to the
  user directly in-chat before doing the rest of the work.
